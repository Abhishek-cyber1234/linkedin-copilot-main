# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

We want this to be a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

**Do:** be kind and patient, respect differing viewpoints, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and take responsibility for your mistakes.

**Don't:** use sexualized language or imagery, troll, insult, or make personal or political attacks, harass anyone publicly or privately, or publish others' private information without their permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers through a private channel (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for how to reach them privately). All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, and may warn or ban contributors whose behavior breaks this code.

The full text, including enforcement guidelines, is at the link above.
